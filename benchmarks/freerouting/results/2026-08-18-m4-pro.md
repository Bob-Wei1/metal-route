# Freerouting comparison — Apple M4 Pro, 2026-08-18

This is the first public result produced by
[`scripts/bench-freerouting.py`](../../../scripts/bench-freerouting.py). It is a
same-input diagnostic smoke test, not evidence that metalroute and Freerouting
have identical routing scope.

## Verdict

No metalroute speedup is established. The only row that passes the quality gate
produced an observed median ratio of **1.09× in Freerouting's favor on bm07**.

- metalroute had lower raw median wall time on bm08, but its task-matched output
  had more violations, so no ratio is reported.
- metalroute also had lower raw wall time on bm06, but the routers reported 97
  vs 98 initial routing tasks, so that row is not workload-comparable.
- Freerouting had the lower median on task-matched bm07 and was also
  better-or-equal on both common post-reload counts, so the observed ratio passes
  the declared gate.
- Across all three boards, Freerouting was no worse in unconnected items and had
  strictly fewer violations.

bm07's spread is high—metalroute 13.462–24.524 s and Freerouting 12.021–15.052
s. All three repetitions favored Freerouting, but the magnitude varied, so 1.09×
is directional smoke evidence, not a precise or general speedup.

## Method

- Same byte-identical DSN for both tools.
- Three repetitions with alternating launch order; median reported.
- One routing worker: `RAYON_NUM_THREADS=1` and
  `--router.max_threads=1`.
- metalroute CPU backend; experimental Metal and adaptive cell-budget overrides
  cleared.
- Freerouting official v2.3.0 profile: fanout, autorouter, and optimizer enabled;
  500 maximum passes; 8 GiB heap ceiling; 30-minute job, 15-minute fanout, and
  10-minute optimizer limits.
- Timed boundary: process spawn through SES creation, including JVM startup.
- Freerouting 2.3.0 then reloads `INPUT.dsn+OUTPUT.ses` and produces the common
  DRC JSON outside timing.
- A ratio is shown only if the initial task count matches and the faster output
  is no worse in both U and V.

Reproduce with externally obtained, hash-matching v2.3.0 inputs:

```sh
cargo build --locked --release -p mr-cli --bin metalroute

python3 scripts/bench-freerouting.py \
  --metalroute target/release/metalroute \
  --freerouting-jar /path/to/freerouting-2.3.0.jar \
  --official-fixture-dir /path/to/freerouting/scripts/benchmark/fixtures/DAC2020_boards
```

The JAR and DSNs are intentionally not vendored. See the
[comparison contract](../README.md), Freerouting's
[v2.3.0 release](https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/releases/tag/v2.3.0),
[benchmark settings](https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/blob/v2.3.0/scripts/benchmark/run-benchmarks.ps1),
[process runner](https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/blob/v2.3.0/scripts/benchmark/lib/BenchmarkRunner.ps1),
and [DRC reload](https://github.com/freerouting/freerouting/blob/v2.3.0/scripts/benchmark/lib/DrcRunner.ps1).
